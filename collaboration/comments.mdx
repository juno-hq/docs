---
title: "Comments"
description: "Leave inline threaded comments anchored to specific text in the document."
---

Comments let you and your teammates discuss specific parts of a spec without disrupting the main conversation. Comments are anchored to text in the document, support threads, and can be promoted into the conversation as context for the agent.

## Overview

Select any text in the document panel and click **Add comment** to start a thread. Your comment is anchored to that text selection — it stays attached even as the document evolves across versions.

Comments are visible to all planspace members with the appropriate access level. They appear as highlights in the document and are listed in the **Reviews & Comments** sidebar.

## Create a comment

1. Select text in the document
2. Click the comment icon that appears, or use the keyboard shortcut
3. Type your comment and submit

Your comment appears as a highlight on the selected text. Other planspace members are notified.

## Threads

Every comment can have replies, forming a thread. Use threads to discuss a specific point without creating noise in the main conversation.

To reply to a comment, click the comment highlight in the document or find the thread in the sidebar.

## Mentions

Type `@` in a comment to mention a teammate. Mentioned users receive a notification in their [For You](/notifications) inbox and the comment appears in their **Mentions** tab.

## Resolve comments

When a discussion is settled, click **Resolve** on the thread. Resolved comments are visually dimmed but not deleted — you can always re-open them or reference the discussion later.

## Add comments to the conversation

Sometimes a comment raises a point that should influence the next version of the spec. Click **Add to chat** on any comment to include it as context in your next message to the agent. The agent will see the comment text and the document passage it's anchored to.

This is a key workflow: the team discusses in comments, and the author funnels the important feedback into the conversation to drive the next iteration.
