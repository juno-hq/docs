## Set up Scott AI

Choose your path based on how you're using Scott AI.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="users" title="I'm setting up for my team">
    You're the first person on your team adopting Scott AI. Here's how to get your team started:

    1. **Sign in** at [tryscott.ai](https://tryscott.ai) or download the [desktop app](https://tryscott.ai/oneshot-download)
    2. **Create your first planspace** — click **New planspace**, name it after the feature or project you're planning, and choose your sandbox environment
    3. **Attach context** — connect your GitHub repo, upload relevant docs, or point to a local folder
    4. **Iterate with the agent** — describe what you want to build. The agent generates a versioned spec from your intent and context
    5. **Invite your team** — click **Share**, add your teammates by email, and assign roles
    6. **Request a review** — when the spec is ready, request reviews from the relevant stakeholders

    <Tip>Start with a real project your team is about to begin. The best way to evaluate Scott is with a plan that actually matters.</Tip>

    **Next steps:** [Sharing](/collaboration/sharing) · [Reviews](/collaboration/reviews) · [Making the most of Scott AI](/getting-started/making-the-most)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="user" title="I'm joining a team">
    Your team is already using Scott AI. Here's how to get up to speed:

    1. **Accept your invite** — check your email for a planspace invite and sign in
    2. **Explore the planspace** — read the document in the right panel, then switch to the **Versions** tab to see how the spec evolved
    3. **Leave comments** — select text in the document and add your feedback. Use `@` to mention teammates
    4. **Install the CLI** — run `npm install -g @tryscott/cli` to push and pull context from your terminal
    5. **Pull context** — run `scott pull` in your repo to link the planspace to your local branch

    **Next steps:** [Context flows](/planspaces/context-flows) · [Comments](/collaboration/comments) · [CLI](/developer-tools/cli)
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="chart-line" title="I'm evaluating Scott AI">
    You want to understand what your team will experience. Here's where to look:

    1. **Read The Scott Method** — [tryscott.ai/method](https://tryscott.ai/method) explains why intent review matters and how it fits into the SDLC
    2. **Open a shared planspace** — ask your team for a public link to see a planspace in action
    3. **Check the For You inbox** — this is your surface for visibility into what's being planned across teams
    4. **See how intent links to code** — [Scott CI](/developer-tools/scott-ci) auto-links PRs back to approved specs

    **Next steps:** [For You](/notifications) · [Scott CI](/developer-tools/scott-ci) · [Making the most of Scott AI](/getting-started/making-the-most)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Install

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion icon="desktop" title="Desktop app (macOS)">
    Download from the [download page](https://tryscott.ai/oneshot-download). Sign in with Google or GitHub. The desktop app adds local sandbox execution and local folder access.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="browser" title="Browser">
    Go to [tryscott.ai](https://tryscott.ai) and sign in with Google or GitHub. The browser app uses cloud sandbox execution and supports Google Drive as a connected source.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion icon="terminal" title="CLI">
    ```bash
    npm install -g @tryscott/cli
    ```

    Then initialize in your repo:

    ```bash
    cd your-project
    scott init
    ```

    See the full [CLI reference](/developer-tools/cli) for all commands.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
