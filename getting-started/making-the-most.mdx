---
title: "Making the most of Scott AI"
description: "How to get the best results from Scott AI across the full development lifecycle."
---

Scott works best when teams align on intent before delegating to agents. Learn more about our philosophy in [The Scott Method](https://tryscott.ai/method).

## Plan

- **Start with a prototype, not a document.** Use your coding agent to build something rough, then run `scott init` and `scott push` to bring that context into a planspace. A working prototype resolves more unknowns than a paragraph of speculation.
- **Attach relevant sources.** Connect your GitHub repo and reference docs so the agent generates specs grounded in your actual codebase.
- **Include code in your plan.** Code blobs and prototype references are first-class content. Plans with implementation snippets give reviewers and agents concrete material to react to.

## Align

- **Share early.** The DRI creates the planspace, iterates with the agent, and shares with the team once there's a prototype worth reacting to.
- **Use comments for specifics.** [Inline comments](/collaboration/comments) are anchored to text in the document. Use them for targeted feedback.
- **Fork to explore alternatives.** [Branch off](/planspaces/context-flows) from an earlier version, try a different direction, and let the team compare working prototypes.
- **Request reviews when aligned.** [Reviews](/collaboration/reviews) create a formal approval record. Request a review once the planspace has a prototype and at least one round of comment-driven iteration.

## Delegate

- **Pull the plan into your branch.** Run `scott pull` to link the planspace to your git branch. This is what enables [Scott CI](/developer-tools/scott-ci) to trace the code back to the intent.
- **Hand off to any agent.** Scott is unopinionated about how you build. Use the approved plan with Cursor, Claude Code, Devin, Copilot, or whatever agent your team prefers.

## Ship

- **Push to GitHub.** Open a PR. [Scott CI](/developer-tools/scott-ci) automatically leaves a comment linking the PR to the approved planspace.

<Note>
**How we work at Scott.** We use planspaces for every feature that involves more than one engineer. The DRI creates the planspace, iterates with the agent, and shares it once there's a prototype worth reacting to. Reviews happen asynchronously — most plans go through 2-3 rounds of comments and one formal review before the DRI runs `scott pull` and delegates to agents.
</Note>
