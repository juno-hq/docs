The **For You** page is your centralized inbox across all planspaces. It surfaces review requests, mentions, and team activity so you can stay aligned without context-switching.

## Overview

For You has three tabs:

| Tab | What it shows |
|-----|--------------|
| **Inbox** | All notifications — invites, forks, review decisions, team activity |
| **Reviews** | Review requests where you're a reviewer or requester |
| **Mentions** | Comments where you're @mentioned |

## Notification types

| Notification | When it fires |
|--------------|--------------|
| **Review requested** | Someone requests your review on a planspace version |
| **Review decision** | A reviewer approves or requests changes on your review |
| **Mention** | Someone @mentions you in a comment |
| **Planspace invite** | You're invited to a planspace |
| **Member joined** | A teammate accepts an invite to your planspace |
| **Fork created** | Someone branches off your work in a shared planspace |

## Mark as read

Notifications are marked as read when you view them. You can also manually mark individual notifications or mark all as read.

## For leaders

The For You page is designed to be the primary surface for engineering leaders and managers. Without opening any individual planspace, you can see:

- Which specs are being designed and who's involved
- Which reviews are pending and which have been approved
- Where your team is collaborating and what decisions are being made

Combined with [public links](/collaboration/sharing), this gives leaders visibility into the design process without disrupting the IC workflow.
